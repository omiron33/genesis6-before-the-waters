## Review notes

The storyboard critic (two rounds) asked for visible people (and was written for the earlier Sethite reading, before the film settled on the fallen-angel reading): the daughters, the sons, an act of violence, and Noe's family boarding. Those notes are declined on purpose: the films in this series show no human figures, faces, moving people or moving human shadows, so people are shown only by what they carry and leave behind. Its chronology notes (no rain until after boarding, a visible boundary around the foretold flood, one-way boarding, cattle with the birds) are applied above.
