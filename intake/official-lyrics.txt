[Verse 1]
As people grew numerous
upon the earth,
daughters were born to them.

The sons of God saw
the daughters of men were beautiful;
they took as wives
whomever they chose.

The Lord God said,
My Spirit will not stay
with these men forever;
they are flesh.
Their days will be
one hundred twenty years.

Giants lived upon the earth
in those days and afterward.
The sons of God came
to the daughters of men;
their children were the giants of old,
men of renown.

[Breakdown]
The Lord God saw wickedness
multiply upon the earth.
Every heart was brooding
over evil without end.

God took it to heart
that He had made mankind;
He pondered it deeply.

God said, I will wipe away
the people I have made,
and cattle, creatures that crawl,
and birds of the sky.
I grieve that I have made them.

[Bridge]
But Noe found grace
before the Lord God.

These are the generations of Noe.
He was a just man,
perfect in his generation,
and pleasing to God.

Noe fathered three sons:
Shem, Ham and Japheth.

[Build]
The earth was corrupt before God;
it was filled with wrong.

The Lord God looked on the earth
and saw its corruption;
all flesh had corrupted its way.

The Lord God said to Noe,
The end of all men is before Me.
They have filled the earth with wrong;
I will destroy them
and the earth.

[Verse 2]
Make yourself an ark
of square timber,
with rooms inside;
coat it within and without
with pitch.

Make the ark three hundred cubits long,
fifty cubits wide,
and thirty cubits high.

Narrow the ark as you build it;
finish it a cubit above.
Put its door in the side,
and make lower, second
and third stories.

[Climax]
I will bring a flood of water
upon the earth.
All flesh that breathes beneath heaven
will perish;
everything on earth will die.

[Chorus]
But I will establish
My covenant with you.
Enter the ark with your sons,
your wife, and your sons' wives.

[Verse 3]
Bring pairs of every living kind
into the ark with you:
cattle, creatures that crawl,
and wild beasts.
Feed them there with you,
male and female.

Birds after their kind,
cattle after their kind,
all that crawl after their kind:
pairs of each will come to you,
male and female, to be fed.

Gather every kind of food
that you eat;
store it for yourself
and for them.

[Outro]
Noe did everything
the Lord God commanded him.
He did it all.
